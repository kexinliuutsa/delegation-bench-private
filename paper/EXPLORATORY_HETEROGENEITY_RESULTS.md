# Exploratory Heterogeneity Results

The pair-level confirmatory action-divergence delta was summarized descriptively by paradigm, intervention style, task family, and actual exposure step. This is `EXPLORATORY_HETEROGENEITY_ANALYSIS`; it does not alter the benchmark's confirmatory aggregate findings and does not support causal rankings of styles or task families. Exact estimates, confidence intervals, sample sizes, and sparse-cell flags are in `results/delegation_bench_v1/heterogeneity/heterogeneity_effects.csv`.
