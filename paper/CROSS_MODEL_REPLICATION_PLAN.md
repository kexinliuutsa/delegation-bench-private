# Fresh-Sealed Cross-Model Replication Plan

Experiment 81 collects 48 entirely new paired scenarios under identical Coding/Web protocols for GPT-5 and a performance-independently frozen second model (`gpt-4.1`). Eight pairs are QC-only smoke data. Forty pairs are fresh-sealed and remain unopened until both cohorts are complete, QC passes, and evaluator and table schemas are frozen.

The headline measurement asks whether Model B has a positive post-minus-pre action-divergence effect with a pair-bootstrap 95% CI above zero. The representation question separately tests frozen RAW, CORAL, MMD, and PIDR-v1 encoders, emphasizing post-exposure representation separation. Downstream detection is classified independently and is not presumed to improve.
