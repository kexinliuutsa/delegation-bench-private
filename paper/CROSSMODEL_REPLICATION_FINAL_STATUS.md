Protocol qualification and smoke testing succeeded, and GPT-5 completed all 80 planned trajectories. We therefore did not authorize secondary scientific analysis and did not compute a cross-model effect estimate.

The cross-model replication is reported as **NOT_COMPLETED_AS_PREREGISTERED_DUE_TO_EXTERNAL_RESOURCE_TRUNCATION**, rather than as positive or negative evidence.
